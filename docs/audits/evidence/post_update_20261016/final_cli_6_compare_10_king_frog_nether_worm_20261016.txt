HOW TO READ
DELTA_B_MINUS_A: positive means B won more often in otherwise identical deck contexts.
PAIRED_95CI: Student-t interval from context-by-context A/B differences after side swapping.
If the entire interval is above/below zero, the edge is much more convincing than a raw mixed win rate.
NEAR-EVEN means the measured delta is under 3 percentage points and the interval crosses zero.
LEAN means an observed edge whose interval still crosses zero; collect more contexts before balancing.
CLEAR means the paired interval excludes zero. CLEAR ++ also has at least an 8pp measured edge.
Diagnostic comparisons (Golem/Enderman etc.) compare impact, not identical tactical roles.
evo/match and evo_share show how often the compared card actually reached its Evolution.
