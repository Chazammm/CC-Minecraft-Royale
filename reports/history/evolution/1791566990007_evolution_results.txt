HOW TO READ
DELTA_EVO_MINUS_BASE isolates the tested Evolution against the same card/deck with no Evolution Slot.
PAIRED_95CI uses a Student-t interval over context-by-context BASE/EVO differences after swapping P1/P2.
reached = percent of EVO matches where the card actually cycled far enough to deploy at least one Evolution.
evo_share = percent of that card's actual plays that were evolved in the EVO variant.
CLEAR BOOST means the paired interval is entirely above zero; CLEAR LARGE BOOST also exceeds +10pp.
A large boost is not automatically overpowered: Evolution Slots are supposed to add power and have cycle/opportunity costs.
Use mixed simulation afterward to judge the resulting card/deck meta.

  evo_compare 100 <card_id>      -- confirm one Evolution
  simulate 1000 mixed            -- overall meta after Evolutions
