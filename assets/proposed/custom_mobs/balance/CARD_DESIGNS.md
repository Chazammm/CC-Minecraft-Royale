The requested cost/HP values below are owner decisions; all balance remains unmeasured. HP/damage use engine units, speed is arena units/s, range is arena units, intervals are seconds. Each card deploys one grounded unit; none attacks flying enemies. `proposals.json` is documentation data, not a `src.cards` extension. ## Netherite Crab — armored melee frontline

**Identity:** low, wide blue-jointed crab with two differently sized armored pincers. Charcoal/brown-violet metal, narrow purple glints and warm claw interiors distinguish netherite from gray rock. The Mob Vote animal was a concept with one enormous claw; our two armored claws are explicitly an original adaptation, not a claim about released vanilla content.

**Chosen ability: Claw Guard.** When at or below 80% HP, an attackable ground enemy is within 6 units, and the 5-second cooldown is ready, show a 0.30-second unprotected pincer windup. Then reduce hostile incoming damage by **35% for 0.80 seconds**, while movement and attacks pause. Cooldown starts when windup begins; no stacking or timer extension. The first guard cannot trigger until 5 seconds after spawn. If the provoking enemy disappears during windup, cancel the guard and spend its cooldown. Damage during guard does not restart it. All hostile hit sources, including spells/reflection, use the same multiplier; no facing checks. Environmental lifetime/tiebreaker effects are not hostile combat hits and bypass this modifier. Counterplay does not depend on guessing a hidden frontal angle.

**Why distinct:** Iron Golem currently costs 5, has 1514.7 HP, 110 damage / 1.5s, speed 4.2 and exclusively seeks buildings. Crab has less raw HP and attacks ground troops. Its visible defensive pause trades offense and movement for a short mitigation window. A hypothetical permanently guarded crab would have 1846.15 damage-equivalent HP, but this is only an unattainable upper bound: the timed stance is not permanent armor.

**Useful against / weaknesses:** can hold a narrow grounded push and absorb a telegraphed heavy hit if timing aligns. Vulnerable to Bat Swarm and Baby Ender Dragon because it cannot retaliate, ranged kiting by Skeleton/Snow Golem, and multi-unit pressure: it has one-target attacks, no splash, reflect or knockback. Minoshroom's 300 hit is reduced to 195 only inside guard and still hits hard. Flanking has no special damage rule in v1; directional armor was rejected because facing and projectile impact orientation are absent from the current combat contract.

**VFX / bot risk:** pincer crossing is the authoritative readable cue; a brief violet plate edge is cosmetic. A bot that assumes Golem-style building focus would misuse it. The bot should value threatened friendly ground troops, guard readiness and lost attack time, not armor color.

## King Frog — grounded displacement support

**Identity:** an enlarged orange vanilla-style, four-legged frog, cream throat, raised eyes, compact gold crown and a short purple back mantle. There are no hands, upright torso or human weapon. The mantle must leave the rear legs visible.

**Chosen ability: Royal Tongue, Fisherman-style hook then melee.** The Frog does **not** repeatedly attack from range12. Its ordinary attack deals40 damage every1.4s at **2.5-unit melee range**. When the tongue cooldown is ready, acquire a valid enemy grounded troop outside melee range and within **12 units**, telegraph for0.25s and pull it toward the Frog until **2.5 units** away (maximum9.5 displacement). Starting a hook attempt spends its4.2s cooldown. A successful pull preserves that target lock and is followed by an ordinary melee attack when the normal attack timer is ready. The hook itself deals **zero damage**; it cannot grant an extra free40-damage hit or reset the basic attack timer. Targets already in melee range are attacked normally without wasting the hook.

Any otherwise legal mobile or stationary enemy **ground unit** may be hooked, including Golem/Minoshroom; the old small-unit whitelist is removed. Buildings, Princess/King towers, flying and water-only units cannot be pulled. Classify by actual entity kind and terrain eligibility, not sprite/name: the current Villager and its Emerald Bank evolution are stationary `kind="unit"`, so they are eligible too. Repositioning must not renew their lifetime or alter emerald production registration.

Revalidate target, distance and terrain at the end of windup. Cancel without damage if the target dies, becomes invalid or leaves hook range; spend cooldown on an attempted hook to avoid rapid retries. The destination is on the original straight line, capped at the last walkable point with2.5-unit separation. If the terrain prevents reaching melee, the Frog must approach normally; it cannot damage from12 units away. No cross-river teleport, perpendicular slide, flight, attack reset or persistent stun. The hook's short pull motion is distinct from the later attack.

**Alternatives:** aura, gameplay leaps, air pulls and tower/building pulls are deferred. Ordinary movement hops are cosmetic. This borrows Fisherman's hook-then-melee role, not every Clash Royale interaction, stat or tower self-pull rule.

**Useful against / weaknesses:** can bring an enemy ranged support into friendly melee reach or pull a tank away from its support. Compared with Snow Golem (3 emeralds,250 HP,26 /1.05s, range18, air coverage,1s slow), Frog now costs the same, has550 HP and trades ranged damage/air coverage for a discrete hook and melee combat. Witch/Skeleton can attack before a hook connects; swarms and air overwhelm its single target. Heavy units are **not pull-immune**: drawing Minoshroom into melee may instead expose the Frog to a300-damage hit. It spawns no minions and provides no aura or healing.

**VFX / bot risk:** tongue line visibly attaches and retracts while pulling; a separate close-range mouth/body motion marks each ordinary attack. The native tongue pose has a narrow extended ribbon between the forefeet, not just an open mouth. Bots must evaluate the pulled target's destination, ally/tower exposure and its retained attack cooldown. Multiple frogs must not repeatedly displace the same victim in one tick.

## Nether Worm — arena-wide ambush assassin

**Identity:** no legs; a compact S-shaped body of dark red/brown/black Nether plates, lava seams, a pointed tail and a large ivory-toothed maw. Contrast this rising silhouette with the long, low, many-legged Centipede. Sparks are separate future effects, not baked into the master.

**Chosen ability: Burrow Ambush.** Cast on valid walkable ground **anywhere in either half**, including behind enemy Princess and King towers. Show a fixed, owner-colored ground warning for **1.4 seconds**, create an **already targetable** emerging unit for **0.6 seconds**, then allow normal attacks and movement. There is no permanent underground entity and no repeat burrow. The first successful hit against a non-structure troop within 2 seconds of becoming active gains **+85 damage, once** (180 total); it does not add a second hit. The window expires if no eligible troop is hit. Ordinary bites deal **95 to units and non-tower buildings**. Only actual `kind="tower"` targets (Princess and King) take **0.50× =47.5**. The+85 opener remains a once-only **unit-target** bonus, so the first eligible troop hit can deal180; no opener is added against buildings or either tower type. This retains the previous special opener while applying the owner’s50% reduction to ordinary tower bites.

**Assassin for economy and support:** prefer a nearby exposed Villager/Emerald Bank or support troop before the existing fallback to structures/towers; do not automatically prefer towers simply because they are closer after emergence. The current Villager costs7, has185 HP and `kind="unit"`; Emerald Bank is its stationary unit evolution with194.25 HP, **not a building entity despite its art**. Both take the full95 normal bite and may receive the once-only180 opener. With no healing/interference, opener+one ordinary hit is275, enough for either. Actual `kind="building"` targets such as Cannon, Pillager Outpost and Nether Portal take95 per hit, without a blanket structure penalty or opener.

Raw normal unit/building DPS is79.17; tower DPS is39.58. Compare Wolf (3 emeralds,330 HP,62 /0.72s, speed10.2): Worm now has the same cost,350 HP, speed9, warning delay and free deployment access. Placement advantage and increased building damage require later balance tests, especially economy harassment. Existing target locks are respected; economic preference applies when acquiring/reacquiring, not a constant mid-attack switch. No new target-selection code is implemented in this package.

**Counterplay:** reposition the support before emergence; deploy a grounded defender beside the warning; use tower coverage, Wolves/Wither Skeleton or aerial damage. A Princess tower deals 80 per hit / 0.95s, King 105 / 0.9s; both may target the emerging worm immediately if eligible. Exact lock timing remains an integration test, not a guaranteed first-shot time. Arrow Volley deals 185, leaving 165 HP from a full-health worm. Falling Anvil deals 549 and can kill it if it stays in the radius, but its **2.7s delay is longer than the worm's 2.0s warning-plus-emergence**: a reactive anvil is not promised to prevent the first bite. The worm does not erase existing tower target locks or force all towers to switch to it.

**VFX / bot risk:** localized ash ring, contracting orange seams, then a rising head. Never an invisible damaging warning. Existing bots clamp ordinary troop casts to their own half; adding only a placement field would not teach them offensive ambushes. Shared validation and enemy-half candidate scoring are required later. See the explicit [state machine and validity rules](../documentation/TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md).

## Ore Centipede — finite sustain tank

**Identity:** low horizontal body with a massive head plate, two ivory mandibles, repeated iron/slate segments, copper joints and many pointed feet. Three visible ore caches: front gold/copper, middle diamond/lapis, rear redstone/emerald. Coal and iron are structural inclusions. These are **three reserves**, not eight separate resource types.

**Chosen ability: Consume Ore.** Start with **3 charges**. When HP ≤70%, missing HP ≥120, not already channeling and cooldown ready, stop attacks/movement and visibly eat one cache over **1.0s**. On successful completion heal **120 HP**, capped at maximum, and consume exactly one charge. The cache empties rear → middle → front. No max-HP increase, armor stat loss, income or ore pickup. All ore types heal identically. Thus the maximum extra healing budget is **360**, giving 2010 cumulative HP only if all three heals actually succeed; maximum displayed HP remains 1650.

Any positive actual hostile damage during the channel cancels it. A failed channel gives no heal and consumes no charge. A **6s cooldown starts on every channel attempt**, including interrupted attempts; therefore damage cannot create a rapid animation/retry loop. Completion after lethal damage is prohibited. If damage and completion occur at the same simulation timestamp, damage/death resolves first. Overheal from any future external heal is capped; zero effective healing must not consume a charge. No resurrection or death-trigger recharge. At most three successful channels per life.

**Useful against / weaknesses:** a single-target tank that can survive pauses between engagements; better recovery than a plain damage sponge, with resources visible to the opponent. Compare Iron Golem's 1514.7 HP / 73.33 DPS / building focus and Minoshroom's 2100 HP / 125 DPS / cost 8. Centipede costs 6, is slow (3.5 vs Mino 3.6) and hits for 65.63 raw DPS before feeding downtime. It cannot heal under uninterrupted damage. Bat Swarm, Baby Ender Dragon, Blaze and sustained ranged fire can deny channels; Minoshroom punishes a stationary feeding unit. No blanket claim that it wins a tank duel has been tested.

**VFX / bot risk:** one selected cache pulses, crumbs travel toward mandibles, then only that cache disappears on success. Cancellation retains the cache. A bot must score missing HP, remaining charges and incoming damage; evaluating only HP would overvalue an empty or constantly interrupted centipede. There is no dependency on mineable arena ore.

## Future balance acceptance before activation

Use deterministic seeded scenarios for single-card trades, support protection, behind-tower worm drops, aerial counters, multiple simultaneous frogs, cancelled feeds and late-game emerald rates.
